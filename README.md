# Baqiyyah – Enduring Deeds, Eternal Rewards ✨

Inspired by the Quranic concept of **“Al-Baqiyat as-Salihat”** (the enduring good deeds), **Baqiyyah** is designed to help you integrate meaningful worship and daily remembrance into your busy modern life.

Baqiyyah is more than just an app; it is your daily spiritual companion. Whether you want to recite the Holy Quran, maintain your morning and evening Azkar, or stay informed about your daily prayers, Baqiyyah brings essential Islamic tools together in one beautifully designed space.

## ✨ Key Features

* 📖 **Holy Quran:** Read and listen to the Quran with multi-language translations and clear audio recitations.
* 🤲 **Authentic Azkar & Duas:** Access a collection of morning, evening, and daily supplications with relevant Hadith references.
* 🕌 **Accurate Prayer Times:** Stay on top of your prayers with precise timetables based on your location, complete with Athan notifications.
* 🧭 **Smart Qibla Finder:** Locate the direction of the Kaaba from anywhere in the world.
* 📿 **Digital Tasbeeh Counter:** Track your daily dhikr with an intuitive, minimalist, and easy-to-use digital counter.
* 🕋 **99 Names of Allah:** Explore the beautiful names of Allah with their meanings and details.
* 🕌 **99 Names of Muhammad (S.A.W):** Explore the beautiful names of Allah with their meanings and details.
* 🔖 **Bookmarks:** Save important Quran verses, Hadith, and other content for quick access later.
* 🕌 **Find Masjid:** Discover nearby mosques and find a convenient place for prayer.
* 🌙 **Islamic Calendar:** View Islamic dates and useful Hijri calendar information.
* 🔔 **Prayer Notifications:** Receive notifications and reminders for your daily prayers.
* 💭 **Daily Spiritual Reminders:** Get gentle reminders throughout the day to encourage remembrance of Allah and consistent worship.

## 🌿 Why Choose Baqiyyah?

* 🚫 **Ad-Free Experience:** Focus entirely on your worship without distracting advertisements or pop-ups.
* 🎨 **Clean & Modern UI:** A beautifully minimalist design crafted for an effortless and peaceful user experience.
* 📚 **Carefully Sourced Content:** Islamic content is provided with relevant references and sources wherever applicable.
* 🌍 **All-in-One Experience:** Essential Islamic tools are brought together in one convenient mobile application.

## 🛠️ Technology

Baqiyyah is an Android application developed as a collaborative project under **ruleno1studio**.

* **Platform:** Android
* **Language:** Kotlin
* **UI:** Jetpack Compose
- **Designing and Development:** Muhammad Waseem & Muhammad Samran
- **Development Environment:** Android Studio + Google AI Studio
## 🌐 Data Sources & APIs

Baqiyyah uses established Islamic APIs and services for different features:

* **AlAdhan** — Prayer times, Hijri calendar, Qibla, and related Islamic data.
* **AlQuran.cloud** — Quran audio and related Quran data.
* **QuranEnc** — Quran translations and word-by-word content.
* **Hadith API** — Hadith data and translations.
